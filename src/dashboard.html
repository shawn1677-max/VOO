Method & caveats

Projections are simulations, not forecasts. Forward paths bootstrap monthly returns from VOO's own 2010–2026 history, which covers one of the strongest stretches in the index's life and contains no 1973, 2000 or 2008 in full. Real outcomes can and do fall outside the shaded band. Dividend figures assume the trailing yield holds and distributions are reinvested. Nothing here is tax or investment advice.
